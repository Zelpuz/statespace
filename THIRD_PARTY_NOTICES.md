## statsmodels

We use statsmodels (`statsmodels.tsa.statespace`) as a reference:

- Some conventions follow it on purpose: output layout, diffuse tolerances, the
  Monahan stationarity transform, optimizer defaults and AIC/BIC.
- The test fixtures in `test/fixtures/` are statsmodels output, generated by
  `test/fixtures/make_fixtures.py`.
